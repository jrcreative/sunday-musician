Sunday Musician helps churches in Portland, Vancouver, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham, and nearby communities find skilled musicians for weekend worship services. Whether you need a last-minute drummer, a regular keys player, or a dependable worship bassist for a few Sundays, we help connect your church with musicians who understand the church setting.

We match skilled musicians with churches that need them — so your team can serve the congregation with confidence, even when your usual volunteers are unavailable.

When one musician is missing, Sunday can feel harder than it should

Church music teams carry a lot. Volunteers travel, get sick, move, burn out, or simply need a week off. And in many Portland-area churches, the worship director is already juggling charts, rehearsal, sound, scheduling, and pastoral realities.

Finding the right substitute is not just about finding someone who can play the parts. You need someone who shows up prepared, listens well, supports the worship leader, and knows how to serve a room rather than perform at one.

Sunday Musician exists for that gap: connecting churches with capable, church-aware musicians who can step in when the weekend needs extra help.

How Sunday Musician works

1. Tell us what you need

Share the date, instrument, service times, location, pay range, rehearsal details, and any expectations for the set.

2. We look for a strong match

We check our Portland-area musician list for players who fit the instrument, schedule, church context, and style of the service.

3. You connect and confirm

When there is a fit, your church can coordinate the final details directly and head into the weekend with one less thing to solve.
