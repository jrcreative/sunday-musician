## Outreach positioning

The pitch should be relational, not transactional. These people are worship leaders first. Lead with the problem Sunday Musician solves for churches and musicians.

### Message to worship leaders

Subject: Portland/Vancouver worship musician network

Hi [Name],

I’m building Sunday Musician here in the Portland / Vancouver area to help churches find trusted worship musicians when they have a gap: last-minute cancellations, special services, fifth Sundays, or giving volunteers a needed week off.

I’m not trying to turn worship ministry into a gig board. I’m trying to build a local network slowly, with people who understand church culture and can serve well.

Would you be open to a quick conversation? I’d love your feedback on what would make this actually useful for worship leaders, and if there are musicians in your circle who might want to be listed for occasional opportunities.

Thanks,
Jeremy

### Message to musicians

Subject: Occasional worship musician opportunities around Portland/Vancouver

Hi [Name],

I’m building Sunday Musician, a local network that helps churches in the Portland / Vancouver area find worship musicians for weekend services, subs, and special events.

The goal is simple: connect churches with musicians who can prepare well, serve the room, and step in when there’s a real need.

If you ever take occasional worship dates, or know musicians who do, I’d love to invite you to join the list.

Thanks,
Jeremy

## Suggested campaign flow

### Week 1: warm referral push

Start with the top 12 names above. Ask for feedback and referrals, not signups first.

Goal:

- 8 conversations requested
- 3 actual calls
- 10 musician referrals

### Week 2: musician invite push

Use referrals, current Sunday Musician musicians, and church contacts to invite musicians directly.

Goal:

- 25 musician invites
- 10 new musician profiles
- Identify the top missing instruments: drums, bass, keys, electric, vocals, production

### Week 3: public social campaign

Post short local invitations on Sunday Musician channels and personal channels.

Example post:

> Portland/Vancouver worship musicians: I’m building a local network to help churches find trusted musicians for weekend services, subs, and special events. If you play drums, bass, guitar, keys, piano, vocals, or help with production, I’d love to get you on the list. This is local, church-aware, and built around serving well.

### Week 4: church-facing follow-up

Circle back to worship leaders with early traction.

Example:

> We’re adding Portland/Vancouver musicians now. If your church ever needs a sub or guest musician, I’d love to have you try the process while we’re still building it locally.

## Notes

- Do not scrape or bulk-message private contact info.
- Use public staff pages, church contact forms, LinkedIn public profiles, Instagram DMs, or warm introductions.
- Prioritize people who can refer multiple musicians over single-player cold outreach.
- Keep the ask small: feedback, referral, or joining the musician list.
