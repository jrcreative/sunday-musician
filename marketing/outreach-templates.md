# Outreach Message Templates — Portland Metro GTM

**Purpose:** Ready-to-use email and DM scripts for each prospect tier.
**Context:** You're serving at a local Portland church. You built Sunday Musician. Lead with that, not the platform.

---

## Tier 1: Warm Relationship (existing connection)

*Use when you have a real relationship with the worship leader — you've served together, grabbed coffee, or have a mutual friend who can make the intro.*

**Subject:** Quick question about something we're building

Hey [Name],

You know how we're always scrambling when someone on the worship team has to miss a weekend.

I've been building something called Sunday Musician with a friend. It helps Portland-area churches find qualified guest musicians and handle the payment side so the church doesn't have to deal with cash or awkward conversations. Musicians set their rate. We handle the logistics.

We have musicians signed up. I'd love to show you how it works and get your honest take. 15 minutes, no pressure. Would that work this week or next?

Either way, hope you're doing well.

Jeremy

---

## Tier 2: Warm Introduction (mutual connection)

*Use when someone you know offered to make an intro. The intro context is your strongest asset — lead with it.*

**Subject:** Intro from [Mutual Friend]

Hi [Name],

[Mutual Friend] mentioned you lead worship at [Church] and suggested I reach out.

Quick background: I'm building Sunday Musician with a friend — it's a local tool that helps churches find and pay qualified guest musicians when they have a gap. Free for churches. We have musicians signed up across Portland and Vancouver and the app handles the payment side so the church doesn't have to.

I'd love 15 minutes to show you how it works and get your feedback as a fellow worship leader. Totally understand if that doesn't fit right now.

Thanks for your time.

Jeremy

---

## Tier 3: Warm-ish (you know of each other, served in overlapping circles, or follow each other on social)

*Use when you're not close but have a legitimate connection point — same conferences, shared circles, mutual city.*

**Subject:** Portland-area worship musician network

Hi [Name],

I've appreciated following your work at [Church]. I'm reaching out because I'm building something I think might be useful for Portland worship leaders.

A friend and I started Sunday Musician — a local network that connects churches with qualified guest musicians when the regular team has a gap. Free for churches. We handle the matching and payment so the worship leader doesn't have to.

We have musicians ready. I'm looking for worship leaders to try it out and tell me what I'm missing. Would you be open to a 15-minute call this month?

Thanks either way.

Jeremy

---

## Tier 4: Cold (public staff page, no existing connection)

*Use for the Priority 1 and Priority 2 prospects from the CSV. Keep it brief, personal, and low-pressure.*

**Subject:** Guest musicians for [Church] — quick question

Hi [Name],

I found your contact on [Church]'s staff page. I'm building a local resource for Portland-area churches and wanted to reach out.

A friend and I started Sunday Musician — it helps churches find qualified guest musicians and handles the payment side, so the worship leader doesn't have to manage cash or awkward conversations. Free for churches.

We have musicians signed up across the metro area. I'd love 15 minutes to show you how it works and get your input.

Totally understand if this isn't on your radar right now.

Jeremy
sundaymusician.com

---

## Tier 5: Follow-up (no response after 5-7 days)

*Use if they didn't respond to the first message. Brief, no guilt, one easy ask.*

**Subject:** Re: Guest musicians for [Church]

Hi [Name],

Circling back in case my note got buried. I'd love 10-15 minutes to show you what we're building and get your honest take as a worship leader. No pitch — genuinely curious what you think would make this useful for churches like yours.

If it's bad timing, no worries at all.

Jeremy

---

## Tier 6: Referral ask (when someone says "not interested but..." or "I can't but maybe...")

*Use after a "no" or "not now." The referral is often more valuable than the original ask.*

**Subject:** Portland worship leaders you'd recommend?

Hi [Name],

Appreciate you taking the time to hear about Sunday Musician. Totally understand if the timing isn't right for [Church] right now.

Quick ask: if there's another worship leader in the Portland area who you think would find this useful — maybe someone at a church that frequently needs guest musicians — I'd love an intro. No rush.

Thanks again.

Jeremy

We handle the matching and payment so the worship leader doesn't have to chase people. Free for churches. I'd love to show you and get your opinion — want to grab coffee sometime?"
